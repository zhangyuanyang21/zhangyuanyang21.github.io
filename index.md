## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Li Yao](https://cs.seu.edu.cn/yaoli/main.htm). I received my Master’s degree from Yantai University, where I was advised by [Prof. Weiqing Yan](https://computer.ytu.edu.cn/info/1018/5200.htm). If you are interested in our research topics or would like to discuss ideas, please feel free to contact me via email or [WeChat](assets/img/wechat.jpg).
